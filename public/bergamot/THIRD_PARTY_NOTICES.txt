Mozilla translation model attachments
-------------------------------------

No translation model weights are bundled in NoriTrans. The extension only
downloads a selected model after an explicit user action. Catalog metadata is
read from Mozilla Remote Settings collection `translations-models-v2`, and the
corresponding compressed attachments are fetched directly from Mozilla's
attachments CDN. NoriTrans does not mirror or redistribute those weights.

Catalog: https://firefox.settings.services.mozilla.com/v1/buckets/main/collections/translations-models-v2/records
Attachments: https://firefox-settings-attachments.cdn.mozilla.net/
Model project: https://github.com/mozilla/translations
